# Precedents

Decided cases, so the same facts get the same verdict. Newest first within each section. Cite the issue number in closing comments when a precedent decides the case.

## Standing rules

- **Spending is not evidence** (2026-08-07). Budget transfers, transfers of partidas, subsidies paid under existing programs and convenios de apoyo presupuestario are the government operating. Discard as «no acredita avance» unless the act itself creates or launches what the promise names.
- **Wrong actor.** Autonomous regulators (SUNASS, OSITRAN…), BCRP, Congress, the judiciary and subnational governments don't execute the national plan. «actor equivocado», even when the topic matches.
- **One company is not a policy.** Qualifying a single firm for an existing incentive (VAT early recovery, lists of goods) is routine, whatever sector the promise is in.
- **Built assets changing hands are not expansion.** Formalizing the free transfer of finished rural-electrification works to a distribution company does not expand electrification.

### Re-reviewed 2026-11-07

### #678 — R.M. D000473-2026-MIDAGRI-DM: discard (2026-11-07)
Extends by 60 days a working group on state food purchases from family farming (Ley 31071). The group was created by R.M. 0132-2026-MIDAGRI for 150 days and had already been extended once by 2026-08-28 (D000339), so it was created before this government; this government changed its membership twice (D000339, D000404, which the matcher missed) and extends it while its proposal is still unwritten. No commitment promises these purchases. A future decree reforming Ley 31071 purchases would still need a matching promise. Lesson: trace the creating act; extensions of inherited bodies are routine.

### #665, #674 — R.M. 670-2026-MINEDU and R.D.E. 203-2026-PRONIED: context, below `t3-2.P08` (2026-11-07)
Rules and school list for the El Niño 2026-2027 rain maintenance program: S/ 460 154 449 from Ley 32744, executed through PRONIED's 2020 subsidy mechanism (R.M. 557-2020-MINEDU). The matcher offered `t3-2.P07` ("Escuelas Seguras", reconstruction of schools >50 % damaged), which it is not. The true neighbour is `t3-2.P08` ("lanzamiento de un Programa Nacional de Infraestructura Educativa Resiliente, con financiamiento mixto… riesgos climáticos y sísmicos"): this is one season, public money only, rains only, an existing mechanism, so below `started`. Lesson: read the plan page. Here the season matters because `t3-2.P08` promises a permanent program; temporariness does not lower promises that are themselves about a crisis (see D.U. 015-2026).

### #663 — D.S. 245-2026-EF: discard (2026-11-07)
New ONP TUPA replacing D.S. 120-2015-EF. The ONP list grows from 23 procedures and one service to 74 procedures, because the TUPA now compiles procedures created by the 2020–2021 pension regulations (a TUPA cannot create procedures, art. 36.3 TUO Ley 27444). Process began in 2025 (MEF report 0192-2025-EF/61.01). Neither progress nor counter-evidence on `t1-3.P12` (eliminate 500 procedures), since nothing is eliminated or newly created. Lesson: a TUPA is a compilation; count the procedures before and after, and read why the count moved.

### #651 — D.S. 018-2026-VIVIENDA: discard (2026-11-07)
Regulation, 26 years late, of Ley 27313: adjudication, readjudication, prescription and chain-of-title procedures by COFOPRI for homes in state housing programs of companies in liquidation (ENACE). No digital, georeferenced or mass procedure. The close neighbours `t3-5.P19`, `t3-5.P20` and `t3-8.P05` all require digital and mass titling; the matcher's `t3-5.P15` (social tariffs) is unrelated. Lesson: "formalización" in the title is not the promised formalization model.

### #650 — D.S. 020-2026-MINEDU: discard, not counter-evidence (2026-11-07)
Removes the 17 projects added in 2025 (D.S. 013-2025-MINEDU) from the PEIP Escuelas Bicentenario portfolio, back to the original 75 in Lima, eight departments and Callao, because the PEIP cannot finish 92 by its 31 March 2028 deadline. PRONIED states it can execute the 17. So the projects move, they are not cancelled, and the PEIP returns to the plan's own scope ("9 regiones", `t3-2.P12`). A first read called it a cut. Lesson: a "removal" can be a transfer; read the considerandos for where the items go. Counter-evidence needs the promised thing to shrink, not to change executor.
